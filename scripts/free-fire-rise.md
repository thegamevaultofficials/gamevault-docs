# How Free Fire Became King After PUBG Ban in India

**Game Vault Documentary #01**

### Hook (0-15 sec)
"In 2020, India banned PUBG - 33 million players cried. But one game silently stole the entire kingdom... Free Fire."

### Part 1: The Underdog (15s - 1min)
- Free Fire launched in 2017 by Garena
- Made for low-end phones - 300MB only!
- While PUBG needed 2GB RAM, Free Fire ran on 1GB
- Nobody took it seriously - called "copy"

### Part 2: The Ban (1min - 3min)
- Sept 2, 2020: PUBG banned by Indian Govt
- 50 million Indian players homeless overnight
- Free Fire downloads jumped 300% in 7 days
- Garena smartly added "Indian" characters like 'K' and events for Diwali

### Part 3: The Empire (3min - 5min)
- 2021: Free Fire became most downloaded game in world
- $1.1 Billion revenue in one year
- Esports prize pool: $2 Million
- Why it won: Low phone + Free + Fast 10-min matches

### Closing Line
"PUBG had graphics. Free Fire had strategy. And strategy always wins."
